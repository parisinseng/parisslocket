From the editor's desk

A little letter from Paris.

I made this little space because I wanted to help. I don't think you have to know everything, or have a lot of money, to care about people.

Sometimes helping starts with sharing a resource, learning someone's story, or connecting someone with what they need. I wanted to keep those things somewhere thoughtful, useful, and easy to find.

For the people who need help, and the people hoping to give it.

with love, Paris ♡
